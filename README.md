RUSH Sportswear — US Sales Analysis
Project Overview

RUSH is a globally renowned sportswear and footwear brand known for its innovative designs and performance-oriented products. As a sales analyst on the US Sales team, I was tasked by the VP of US Sales with analyzing raw company sales data to uncover trends and insights that support company leadership's understanding of the market and identification of growth opportunities.

Business Context

RUSH's raw sales data is stored across three tables:

TABLE_PRODUCTS — product-level details (e.g., product category, type)
TABLE_RETAILER — retailer and location details
TABLE_SALES — transactional sales data (units sold, revenue, sales method, date, etc.)

Field-level definitions are documented in the accompanying data dictionary. The data is raw and required cleaning prior to analysis (deduplication, type correction, handling of missing/erroneous values, standardization of categorical fields, etc.).

Objectives
Clean and prepare the raw data for analysis (EDA).
Answer the VP's core business questions:
What product category had the highest sales (in dollars) in 2021, and how much did it sell?
What state had the highest sales (in dollars) of women's products in 2021, and how much was it?
What state had the highest sales (in dollars) of men's products in 2021, and how much was it?
What retailer purchased the most units in 2021? In 2020?
Identify additional business-relevant trends and insights (e.g., seasonality, retailer performance, regional patterns, sales method effectiveness) to help leadership understand the market and spot growth opportunities.
Communicate findings to the VP and sales team in a concise 3–5 minute presentation.
